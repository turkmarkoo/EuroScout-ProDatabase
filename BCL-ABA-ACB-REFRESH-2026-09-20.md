# BCL / ABA / ACB roster refresh — 20 September 2026

The official roster overlay was refreshed into current-profile metadata without replacing historical statistics.

## Applied

Every matched BCL, ABA and ACB overlay player now exposes:

- `currentRosterSeason: 2026/27`
- `currentClub`
- `currentRosterSource`
- `currentRosterProfile`
- the existing stable provider identity and birth year

This keeps the current club separate from historical statistical stints and makes the official profile link available to the player drawer/profile.

## Photos

Existing provider photos are retained. The current overlay still has no confirmed image URL for 145 ABA players, 76 ACB players and 193 BCL players. I did not populate guessed URLs. BCL has stable FIBA person codes for many records, so those candidates need an asset-by-asset availability check before adding them; an absent image in the roster payload is not proof that the player has no photo.
