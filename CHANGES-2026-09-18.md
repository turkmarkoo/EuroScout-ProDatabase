## Where things are stored
- `euroscout:sessions:v1` — finished sessions, synced with the other app data.
- `euroscout:session:active:v1` — the running session, this device only.
- `euroscout:shortcuts:v1` — shortcut bindings per account (cloud copy, editors only);
  everyone also keeps a local copy under `mt:shortcuts:v1:euroscout:<email>`.
- Each reviewed player: `_workflow.viewings[]` gains `sessionId`, `status`, `competition`;
  `_workflow.noteTimes` holds when each note was written.

## Tunables (top of `euroscout-sessions.js`)
`DWELL_MS` (5 s to count as viewed), `FAM` (familiarity weights), `FAM_BANDS`.

## Fixtures
- EuroLeague (380 games) and EuroCup (224) are fetched by the app from `api-live.euroleague.net`, cached for 6 hours in `euroscout:fixtures:v1` (this device). Scores fill in as games are played.
- API teams are matched to clubs by code **and** name — codes get reused (BUR was Burgos, now Tofas Bursa). Anything unmatched is listed under “things to check” on Scouting log → Upcoming games.
- Other leagues: add a block to `data/fixtures-2627.js` in the `*_games.txt` pipe format (`id|round|date|HOME|hs|AWAY|as|time|venue`, empty scores = not played).

## Straight into the matchup (`euroscout-matchup-link.js`, new)
- Link: `#matchup`, `#matchup=<teamA>~<teamB>`, `#matchup=<teamA>~<teamB>~YYYY-MM-DD`. A team is a club key (`aba|COL`) or its name.
- From the hub, for a frame that is already open: `postMessage({type:'hub:goto', view:'matchup', a, b, date}, 'https://euroscout.mtscouting.com')`.
- A running session reopens the matchup on load.
- Ctrl+M opens the matchup from anywhere in EuroScout — administrator only.

## Phase 2 brief (20/09/2026)
New: `euroscout-workspace.js`, `euroscout-signals.js`, `euroscout-quickstats.js`, `euroscout-quickstats.css`.
Changed as well: `euroscout-ui.js` and `euroscout-workflow.js` (names instead of email addresses).

- **Scout workspace** (`ESWorkspace.visible()`): sessions, Scouting log, Scouting history, Team log and shortcuts show for the administrator and for hub roles listed in `WORKSPACE_ROLES` (top of the file). Everyone else keeps notes, reports and Quick Stats.
- **Names, never emails** (`ESWorkspace.nameOf`): the name DragonsHub sends is remembered in `euroscout:names:v1` (synced); otherwise the part before the @ is tidied into a name. The access-management screen still lists addresses — that is what it manages.
- **Signals** (`ESSignals.RULES`): Coming out of college · Rookie in Europe · New team · Draft prospect · U22 with a real role. "Breakout season" is written but silent: it needs a previous-season line and the database holds 2025/26 only. At most three show.
- **Quick Stats**: 📊 Stats in the matchup header (key T). Keys 1–8 switch category, Esc closes, ↑/↓ keep working and the panel follows the player.
- **Scouting history replaces the Viewing log** on the profile. A note edit no longer creates a history entry; finishing a session does. Note revisions are summarised one line per day.
- Matchup header: dominant hand (set it in the profile's edit form) and signals added.
